What this website collects

This website is a static brochure plus a set of forms. It has no user accounts, no login and no advertising or analytics trackers.

Information you send us

If you use the enquiry or quote forms, we receive whatever you type in them: your name, contact details, project location and the description you provide.

  • Contact forms: name, email address, phone number and your message.
  • Product inspection request: the product you are interested in, the approximate area, and a description of the damage and any previous repair attempts.

While the site sends form details to your own email application so you can review and send the message, nothing is transmitted to a server we control until you send it. TODO if a server-side form handler is added later, this section and the cookies page must be updated to match.

Information collected automatically

The site stores one item in your browser's session storage, a flag recording that you have seen the loading animation, so the animation is not shown again during the same visit. This never leaves your browser.

How your information is used

  • To reply to your enquiry or quote request.
  • To send technical datasheets you have asked for.
  • To prepare a quotation or technical recommendation.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.
