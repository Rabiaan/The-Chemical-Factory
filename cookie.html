Cookie Policy

Short version

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and has no tracking of any kind. The only thing stored is a single flag in your browser's session storage, used so the loading animation is not shown twice in one visit.

What is actually used

Session storage — cf-seen

When the loading animation first runs, the site writes a value of 1 under the key cf-seen in session storage. This is used only to decide whether to play the animation on the next page of the same visit.

  • Type: strictly necessary, first-party.
  • Lifetime: cleared when you close the browser tab or window. It is never written to disk permanently.
  • Contains: no identifier, no personal data, no fingerprint.

Local and quote state

If you open the product inspection questionnaire and then move to the quote form, the answers you entered are carried across in session storage so you do not have to retype them. This is stored in your browser only and is never transmitted unless you send the form yourself.

Third-party cookies

Google Fonts

The site's headings and body text use Google Fonts. When those fonts load, Google's servers see your IP address and browser user agent. Google states it does not use font requests to build an advertising profile, but this is a transfer of data to a third party and it is disclosed here rather than left implicit.

Google Maps

The contact page embeds a map. Google may set cookies and process your data when it loads. TODO if the map is switched to a static image, or removed, update this section.

Turning things off

Session storage cannot be blocked by a privacy setting, but it can be cleared at any time through your browser's site-data controls. Blocking third-party requests stops the font and map requests, in which case the page falls back to system fonts.
