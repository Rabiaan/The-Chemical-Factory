Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Solutions.

Product & Application

Elastomeric liquid coatings cure into a seamless, highly elastic UV-resistant rubberized skin ideal for exposed rooftops and complex flashings. Bituminous torch-on or self-adhesive sheet membranes provide thick, durable multi-ply barrier protection for subterranean foundation walls and ballast roofs.

Warranties

Our systems come with a comprehensive 10 to 25-year structural waterproofing warranty depending on the system selected (such as Polyurea, Crystalline, or Multi-layer Polyurethane) when applied by certified engineers.

Site Inspection

We utilize non-destructive electronic vector mapping (EFVM), infrared thermal imaging cameras, and pin-type digital moisture meters to map exact water migration pathways before recommending chemical injection or coating remediation.

Curing & Weather

Fast-cure Polyurea systems cure in seconds to minutes, allowing foot traffic within 1-2 hours. Acrylic and Polyurethane liquid membranes typically require 12 to 24 hours between coats under standard ambient humidity conditions.

Maintenance

Yes! Our high-adhesion primers and flexible 2-component polymer slurry systems can bond directly to properly prepared porcelain tiles, concrete decks, and metal roofing without costly tear-offs.
