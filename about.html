Our Story

Engineered Protection Backed by 9+ Years of Field Chemistry

The Chemical Factory is a leading provider of construction chemicals, waterproofing solutions and specialized engineering services in Pakistan. With over 9 years of industry experience, we deliver innovative, durable and cost-effective systems for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

Our expertise spans waterproofing systems, structural strengthening, epoxy flooring, PU injection, heat insulation, concrete repair and rehabilitation, CFRP systems, expansion joints, shotcrete and soil nailing, and protective coatings. We collaborate with renowned local and international manufacturers to ensure the highest standards of quality and performance.

Customer satisfaction, technical excellence and long-term reliability are at the core of everything we do. Engineering stronger structures, delivering lasting solutions.

Our 4-Step Process

A disciplined, engineer-led workflow from first inspection to final handover.

01

Free Site Inspection

Certified engineers map leaks, moisture, and substrate condition using non-destructive diagnostic tools.

02

System Specification

We specify the exact chemical system, membrane thickness, and coverage rate with transparent itemized pricing.

03

Certified Application

Trained chemical crews prepare, prime, and apply the system with strict quality control at every coat.

04

Testing & Handover

Flood testing verifies the seal, then we hand over the as-built system documentation and a maintenance guide.
